Backspatter Gunshot
Designed by: D. Attinger, D van Ryswyk, Ricky Faflak, Yu Liu
Performed by: D. Attinger, D van Ryswyk, Ricky Faflak

Image Scale: 600 dpi (42.3 micrometer per pixel)

Environmental conditions: 
Room Temperature (in degree C): 18.0 +/- 2
Relative Humidity (%): 70 +/-5

Blood Properties: Swine blood with heparin anti-coagulant, hematocrit measured twice at 37% and 39%, drawn one day before the experiment
Blood Temperature: Room temperature

All coordinate measurements are taken from the bullet hole

Blood source: foam board cavity filled with blood - 2 inch diameter x 5mm depth
Blood source position: vertical, ~57cm from ground

Target: Premium Poster board, Walmart UCreate, smooth side
Target position: vertical
Blood Volume (mL): 10+/-1 


Gun: Smith and Wesson handgun 9mm 
Bullet: AE9AP (round)

muzzle gases: screened out, to minimize interaction with spatter
Distance from target to blood source (cm): 30
Distance from gun to blood source (cm): ~150
